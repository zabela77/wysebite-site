WYSEBITE PRIVACY POLICY
Version 2026-10-19

WyseBite has no user servers and no user accounts in the cloud. Your data stays on your iPhone unless you choose to send it. We don't sell personal data, we don't show ads, we don't use analytics or tracking tools, and we don't track you across other apps or websites.

1. What stays on your phone
• Location: used only while you use the App, to show what's nearby. It isn't stored or sent to us.
• Account: your email address, your phone number if you add it, and, if you continue with Apple, the account ID Apple provides and the name you let it share. If you use Apple's "Hide My Email", we only get a relay address. Kept in your phone's Keychain.
• Profile: your name, profile photo, and diet and coffee preferences.
• Logbook, gold stars, notes, photos, towns and lists you add.
• Friends: your friends' names, friend codes and photos, and the encryption key for private sharing (in your phone's Keychain).
• AI results: scores, menu readings and categories made on your device by Apple Intelligence. Most of these are encrypted on your phone and left out of backups, because they can be made again.
• Only if you turn on "Help improve recommendations" (it starts off; you can change it at sign-up or any time in Profile): a log of how you use the App, such as places you open, save or rate. This log stays on your phone. If we ever offer to upload it, we'll ask for your separate permission first.

2. What leaves your phone
• Finding places: the App asks Apple Maps for places in the area you're viewing, and visits restaurants' public websites and Wikimedia Commons to read menus, photos and logos. Places from Overture Maps and OpenStreetMap are built into the App, so they need no lookup. For the safety layer and My Town spots, it asks OpenStreetMap, public city data sites and the US National Weather Service about that area. These services receive a normal web request from your device (which includes your IP address) and the area being looked up; we add no personal information. Place details from Apple Maps are kept only in memory while the App is running; places Apple Maps finds that the built-in maps don't have are kept on your phone for up to 7 days so the map loads faster, then deleted.
• Continue with Apple: Apple confirms who you are. We never receive your Apple password.
• Sharing with friends: a share contains only what you chose to send, plus your display name, profile photo, friend code and your gold-star ratings for the places in it. Private shares are end-to-end encrypted.
• Feedback you send us: "Score off?", the envelope button and "Contact us" open an email in your mail app, which you can read before you send it. A "Score off?" email contains the place's name and location, the App's score, your verdict and your own stars if you gave any, the App version, and a sealed code describing how the score was worked out. We receive your email address when you send it. We use feedback emails only to answer you and to improve scores, and we delete them when they're no longer needed.
• Contacts: if you pick a contact to invite, Apple's contact picker shares only that one contact with the App, to fill in the message. The App can't read your address book.
• Your iCloud backup (controlled by you in iOS Settings) may include the App's data.

3. Apple Intelligence
AI features run on your device. Text about places, menus and your diet choices is processed on your iPhone and isn't sent to us.

4. Your choices and rights
• Turn optional data collection on or off at any time: Profile › Privacy & data.
• Download a copy of your data: Profile › Privacy & data › Download my data.
• Delete everything: Profile › Privacy & data › Delete all my data.
• Location and camera access can be changed in iOS Settings at any time.
Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, or California under the CCPA), you have rights to access, correct, delete and port your data and to object to processing. Because your data is stored on your device, you can use most of these rights yourself in the App. For feedback emails you've sent us, or anything else, contact us.

5. Children
The App isn't for children under 13. We don't knowingly collect data from them.

6. Security
Your data is kept in the App's private storage on your device, and sensitive items in the Keychain. Saved scoring files are encrypted (AES-GCM). Private shares use end-to-end encryption (Curve25519 and AES-GCM).

7. Changes
If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we'll show it to you and ask you to accept it again.
