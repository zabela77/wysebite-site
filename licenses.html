DATA SOURCES AND LICENSES

Places and map data
• OpenStreetMap: © OpenStreetMap contributors. Available under the Open Database License (ODbL 1.0): openstreetmap.org/copyright. The App includes OpenStreetMap's US food places and its airport maps, built from Geofabrik's extracts; WyseBite kept only named food places and the tags it uses. These files are available under the same license: ask us for a free copy of them and of how they were made: gobiddygo@gmail.com.
• Overture Maps Foundation: places data from the Overture Maps Places release (2026-09-23), under the Community Data License Agreement – Permissive, Version 2.0 (cdla.dev/permissive-2-0), with some records under the Apache License 2.0 (apache.org/licenses/LICENSE-2.0) or CC0 1.0. Overture's data comes from Meta, Microsoft, Foursquare, BrightQuery, AllThePlaces and others. The data is provided "as is" without warranties.
• Apple Maps: map display, place details and Look Around, under Apple's terms. Apple's place details are kept only in memory; places Apple Maps finds that the built-in maps don't have are kept on your phone for up to 7 days so the map loads faster, then deleted.
• Wikimedia Commons and Wikidata: some place photos, credited to their authors under their licenses.
• Whatcom County and King County (Washington) public parcel records: what the land at a place's spot is used for, to leave out listings that sit only on housing or lodging. Public records, read on your phone.

Safety information
• Public city open-data portals (police incident data) and the US National Weather Service (alerts), shown with their sources.

Scores
• WyseBite's scores are made on your iPhone by Apple Intelligence from the sources above and each restaurant's own public website. They aren't reviews and aren't endorsed by any data provider.

Changes WyseBite made to the Overture data
• Kept food and drink places only, and stored them in WyseBite's own file format inside the App.
