Support

Questions, a score that looks off, a closed place, or a privacy request? Email gobiddygo@gmail.com. In the app you can also tap Score off? on any place, or use Profile › Privacy & data › Contact us.

To delete your data: Profile › Privacy & data › Delete all my data. Your data is stored on your phone, so this removes it.

OpenStreetMap data in the app is available under the ODbL; ask us for a free copy of the app's OpenStreetMap file and how it was made. See data sources and licenses.
